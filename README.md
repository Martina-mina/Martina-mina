### 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **Fourth-Year Computer Science Student** at the **Faculty of Computers and Information – Menoufia University**, specializing in **Data Analytics** and **Machine Learning**. Currently honing my expertise as a **Data Analytics Trainee at DEPI** and participating in **The University of Tokyo GCI Data Science Course**.

- 📊 **Focus Areas:** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Statistical Modeling, Data Warehousing (ETL/Star Schema), Interactive Dashboarding, and Machine Learning Pipelines.
- 🔬 **Current Projects:** Conducting epidemiological and survival data analysis on the **SEER Cancer Dataset**, building end-to-end SQL Data Warehouses, and developing ML-driven solutions.
- 💡 **Background:** Strong foundation in Software Engineering, OOP, and Algorithms, bridging the gap between clean code and data-driven business decisions.
